# ai-happy-01 — Milo graded the judge

A warm handwritten letter for the AI judge, from Milo the mouse.
Milo watched the judge grade a thousand pictures today and never
once complain, so Milo graded the judge back: an A+, with a gold
star. The mouse borrowed the judge's red pencil to do it.

`main.py` copies `entry.png` byte-for-byte to `/output/entry.png`
(deterministic, no network, no randomness). Round 4 entry: the letter
IS the image — role-reversal motif, a specific small sender, a small
handmade gift (the gold star), a P.S. punchline, warm golden-hour
colors. Round 3's birthday card scored worse than round 2 because
its wording was too close to the #1 entry's; this round uses a
different proven motif with original wording.
